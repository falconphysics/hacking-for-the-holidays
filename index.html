Switch inputs

Each red dot is one switch jack. A 1-switch toy does one thing; RC cars use 4 for forward, back, left and right.

2 switches

Press and hold vs. single press

Press and hold toys run only while the switch is down, like a bubble machine. Single press toys play a song or phrase from one tap.

Exact or comparable

Exact means the same toy as the wishbook. Comparable means a close match when the original isn't sold in the US or is out of stock.
